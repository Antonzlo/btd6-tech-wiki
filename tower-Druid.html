Path 2

  1. 1. Thorn Swarm$250total $250

    Shoots 8 thorns per shot instead of 5.

  2. 2. Heart of Oak$350total $600

    Attacks convert Regrow Bloons into normal Bloons. Gains 1% pierce for every life gained from the starting value after the upgrade. Maxes at 100% and diminishes if lives are lost.

  3. 3. Druid of the Jungle$1 050total $1 650

    Calls a vine from the ground to entangle and crush Bloons one at a time, leaving behind a deadly thorn pile.

  4. 4. Jungle's Bounty$4 900total $6 550

    Vines create stronger thorn piles. Druid generates cash and lives at the end of each round, plus extra cash per Banana Farm near the Druid. Vine Crush Ability: Vines grab many Bloons at once, following Druid's target priority.

  5. 5. Spirit of the Forest$35 000total $41 550

    Grows thorned vines along the path that deal constant damage and bonus damage to ceramics. Vines nearest the Spirit of the Forest do more damage. Activated ability grabs more Bloons.
