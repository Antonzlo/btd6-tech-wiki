Paragon

A paragon gets a degree from 1 to 100 from accumulated power. Power comes from pops, cash spent and sacrificed upgrades, each source with its own cap.

Data: 57.4 (Mod Helper export, 2026-11-04)

Where power comes from

  • max from pops125 00049.0%
  • max from cash80 00031.4%
  • max from T550 00019.6%

Degree 100 needs 200 000 power, and these sources cap out at 255 000. None of them alone reaches 200 000.
