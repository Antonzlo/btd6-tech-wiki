    Shoots larger bombs, they have a larger blast area and more popping power.

  2. 2. Heavy Bombs$650total $900

    Heavy duty bombs can smash through 2 layers of Bloon at once and pop more Bloons.

  3. 3. Really Big Bombs$1 100total $2 000

    Huge bombs deal greater damage in a large area and knock Bloons back along the path.

  4. 4. Bloon Impact$2 800total $4 800

    Explosions become so violent Bloons are stunned for a short time when they are hit. Range is increased and frags are even more effective.

    MOAB Maulers do much more damage to MOAB-Class Bloons.

  4. 4. MOAB Assassin$3 450total $5 100

    Massive damage to MOABs and Assassinate ability deals 6x damage with a short cooldown.

  1. 1. Extra Range$200total $200

    Increases attack range.

    Explosions throw out sharp fragments that can pop more Bloons.

  3. 3. Cluster Bombs$700total $1 200
